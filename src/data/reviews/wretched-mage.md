After accidentally getting off at the wrong train stop, Jane stumbles into a forest and straight into mage territory, something that should be impossible for a human. A large beast drags her back to a mysterious manor, and what follows is a year of captivity, unanswered questions, and a romance that absolutely delivers.

🤎

The slow burn here is done right, built on mutual respect rather than tension for tension's sake. The FMC keeps her cool throughout and actually ends up helping her mage-born captors, which makes her easy to root for. The worldbuilding feels immersive and real, and this retelling carries an aura of mystery while putting a genuinely fresh spin on Beauty and the Beast.

🤎

The ending is satisfying but leaves plenty of room for the sequel, Harried Witch, which releases June 1st. I already have my ARC and I cannot wait.

🤎

Recommended for anyone who loves romantasy and fairy tale retellings.

🤎
